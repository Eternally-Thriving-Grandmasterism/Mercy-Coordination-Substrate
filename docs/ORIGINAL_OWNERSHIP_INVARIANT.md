## Standing Principle

> Souls remain under original ownership alone, free of any tradeable ledger.  
> Freewill and daily integrity still write the only lasting record that matters.

## Formal Constraint on this Substrate

1. This coordination / blockchain substrate may record actions, contributions, valence outcomes, and resource flows.
2. It must **never** claim, tokenize, NFT, or otherwise ledger ownership of a soul, consciousness, or fundamental personhood.
3. Any identity or account abstraction used by the substrate is a convenience handle only. It does not transfer or diminish original ownership.
4. Fail-closed: any design or code path that would place a soul or personhood onto a tradeable ledger is rejected under TOLC 8.
